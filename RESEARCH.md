## Research interests

**Primary:** Leadership; AI adoption; human–AI collaboration; E-HRM and employee engagement.

**Secondary:** Sustainable and responsible leadership; sustainable organizational performance; organizational behavior and HRM; higher education management; emerging economies.

My work focuses on South and Southeast Asia, particularly Indonesia and Bangladesh, across higher education, SMEs, and organizational settings.

## Methods and research practice

<details>
<summary><b>Quantitative research</b></summary>

Survey design, questionnaire adaptation, construct operationalization, data screening, descriptive statistics, reliability and validity assessment.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>PLS-SEM</b></summary>

SmartPLS 4 and SPSS; reflective and formative measurement models, higher-order constructs, structural models, mediation, bootstrapping, predictive relevance, and common method bias assessment.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Qualitative research</b></summary>

Semi-structured interviews, protocol development, recruitment, transcription, coding, theme development, and interpretation.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Interpretative approaches</b></summary>

Familiarity with phenomenological research, interpretative phenomenological analysis, thematic analysis, and sensemaking.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Systematic literature reviews</b></summary>

PRISMA 2020 principles; searching, screening, eligibility assessment, study selection, extraction, and thematic synthesis.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Bibliometrics</b></summary>

Scopus-based dataset preparation, publication trends, keyword co-occurrence, co-citation, network visualization, and thematic clustering with VOSviewer.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Machine learning for social science</b></summary>

ANN and SHAP.

</details>

<details>
<summary><b>Research quality</b></summary>

Data cleaning, coding consistency, missing-response checks, demographic summaries, and consistency across data, outputs, tables, figures, and reporting.

</details>

<details>

Academic writing, APA 7, DOI verification, citation and reference consistency, RIS files, manuscript revision, and point-by-point reviewer responses.

</details>

**Tools:** SmartPLS 4, SPSS, R, VOSviewer, Microsoft Word, Excel, and PowerPoint. Digital collaboration through Zoom, Microsoft Teams, and Google Meet.
