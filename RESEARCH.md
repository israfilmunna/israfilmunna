# Research agenda and methodological profile

[← Back to profile](README.md)

## Research identity

My research is positioned at the intersection of leadership, organizational behavior, human resource management, digital transformation, and artificial intelligence. I focus on organizational problems in emerging-economy settings, particularly Bangladesh and Indonesia, and aim to produce context-sensitive evidence with relevance for theory, management practice, and public policy.

## Core research streams

### 1. Digital leadership and AI adoption

This stream examines how leaders shape technology adoption, employee readiness, trust, and responsible use of artificial intelligence. Current interests include:

- digital leadership and employee digital readiness;
- AI adoption intention and actual use;
- algorithmic transparency and perceived fairness;
- trust in AI-based performance evaluation;
- responsible and human-centered technology implementation.

### 2. Leadership and organizational behavior

This stream studies how leadership influences employee and organizational outcomes across different institutional and cultural contexts. Topics include:

- transformational, responsible, sustainable, crisis, and toxic leadership;
- employee engagement, psychological safety, trust, and creativity;
- career development and knowledge sharing;
- sensemaking under harmful or uncertain leadership conditions.

### 3. Human resource management and the future of work

This stream focuses on changing employment relationships and HR systems, including:

- electronic human resource management;
- workplace flexibility and work–life balance;
- Generation Z organizational commitment;
- employee engagement and perceived organizational support;
- AI-enabled performance evaluation and sustainable HRM.

### 4. Higher education management

This stream investigates leadership, technology, and institutional effectiveness in universities. Current topics include:

- lecturer career development;
- digital leadership and academic performance;
- student adoption of generative AI;
- university–community engagement;
- academic productivity and research capacity development.

### 5. Values, spirituality, and sustainability

This stream explores how ethical, religious, and sustainability-oriented values shape organizations. Current interests include:

- Islamic spirituality and organizational leadership;
- responsible leadership and corporate social responsibility;
- decent work and SDG 8;
- sustainable organizational development;
- culturally grounded leadership theory in South and Southeast Asia.

## Research principles

My research practice is guided by the following principles:

1. **Theory before technique:** analytical tools should answer a theoretically meaningful question rather than replace conceptual reasoning.
2. **Context sensitivity:** models developed in dominant Western settings should be examined critically before application in emerging economies.
3. **Methodological transparency:** research decisions, limitations, and analytical procedures should be documented clearly.
4. **Ethical data use:** datasets, instruments, and code should be shared only when participant protection, institutional approval, and copyright conditions permit.
5. **Reproducibility:** analysis files, codebooks, search protocols, and reporting templates should be made reusable whenever feasible.
